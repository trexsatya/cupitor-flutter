Privacy Policy for Cupitor

Last updated: April 10, 2026

Overview

Cupitor is a contextual dictionary and reading app. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is used.

Microphone / Speech Recognition

Cupitor uses your device's microphone solely for voice word lookup. When you tap the microphone button, your speech is processed by the on-device speech recognition service provided by your operating system (Google Speech Services on Android, Apple Speech on macOS/iOS).

  • Audio is not recorded, stored, or transmitted by Cupitor.
  • The recognized text is used only to look up the word in a dictionary within the app.
  • Microphone access is only activated when you explicitly tap the microphone button.

Data Collection

Cupitor does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Specifically:

  • No analytics or tracking.
  • No user accounts or sign-in.
  • No data sent to third-party servers by the app itself.

Local Storage

The app stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Bookmarks and last visited URL.
  • Scroll positions for recently visited pages.
  • User preferences (e-ink mode, language selection).

This data never leaves your device.

Network Access

The app uses internet access to load web pages and PDF documents that you navigate to. It does not send any data to servers other than the URLs you choose to visit.

Third-Party Services

When you use voice input, the speech recognition is handled by your device's built-in service (Google or Apple). Their respective privacy policies apply to that processing.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact the developer via the app's Play Store listing.
